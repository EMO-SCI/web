  • Fall 2024-2025

    Our Humble Beginnings

    The idea for EMO-SCI was developed by Konstantin, inspired by Eva Illouz’s "The Emotions against Democracy" and by Salim’s findings that online scientific discourse is highly emotional. Konstantin shaped the project with Stefan, who framed it as a science-distortion problem, and Achim, who introduced the focus on multi-turn discussions; Serena joined as the French partner bringing expertise in argument mining. The consortium wrote the proposal in early 2025 and received acceptance in fall 2025.
